---
name: Shared school print profile
description: Product requirement for school identity and contact details across printed school documents.
---

School identity and contact details should be editable in one shared profile, then selectable for ID cards, marksheets, certificates, and fee receipts. Include principal and director names and mobile numbers, address, and custom school fields.

**Why:** The user wants complete and consistent school details on all printouts, rather than a partial set that must be maintained separately.

**How to apply:** Keep school details in the shared school profile, persist them as school defaults, and confirm each printout renders its selected fields.
